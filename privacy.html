Privacy Policy — ProRa

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Who processes data

ProRa is operated under the name Raum Security, a project run by an individual. Responsible individual: Brașoveanu Ciprian, Romania; privacy email: ciprian.raum@gmail.com. Available contact: Discord raum.c.

2. Data processed

  • Discord IDs for servers, users, roles and channels, and display names where required by features.
  • License and configuration settings, including licensed server IDs and log-channel IDs.
  • Moderation events: deleted or edited messages (including content available to the bot), invite links, images, sanctions, member joins/leaves, and role or channel changes, depending on settings and permissions.
  • For requested AI summaries: recent messages from the selected channel, potentially including text excerpts, display names and timestamps.

ProRa does not need or request users’ Discord passwords.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is used for license activation, moderation, security, logging, support and AI summaries. Server administrators should provide appropriate notices to their members.

4. AI summaries and third parties

The operator has enabled Groq Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for inference API requests; this limits provider-side logging of request and response content, but does not necessarily eliminate technical metadata. When the /rezumat command is used, ProRa reads a configurable number of recent messages (for example, 100–1000) and sends excerpts to the Groq API to generate a summary. In the known implementation, message text is shortened and URLs are removed before submission; this does not guarantee anonymization. Data may contain personal information shared by members. Discord and the hosting provider may process data under their respective policies.

5. Moderation logs

Depending on configuration, logs are posted to a Discord channel selected by the server administrator. Access to and retention of those Discord messages are controlled by server administrators and Discord’s rules.

6. Retention

Under the stated policy, license, configuration and partnership data is retained until the partnership ends, subject to statutory retention requirements; upon license revocation, local license and configuration data no longer needed is removed. Image-spam detection data may be held temporarily in memory. Discord log messages remain until deleted by authorized administrators or according to Discord policies.

7. Recipients and security

Data may be accessible to the ProRa operator, authorized server administrators, Discord, the hosting provider and Groq when AI is used. Access is intended to be restricted through Discord permissions and technical credentials protected.

8. Your rights

Where provided by GDPR, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, as applicable, and lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority (in Romania, ANSPDCP). Send requests to the email address below; proportionate identity verification may be required and responses are provided within GDPR deadlines, normally one month. Alternative contact: Discord raum.c. Requests email: ciprian.raum@gmail.com. For deletion of messages in server channels, you may also need to contact the server administrator.

9. Minors and updates

Use of ProRa must comply with Discord’s age requirements. This policy may be updated to reflect service changes and legal requirements. Material changes will be announced through the website or available contact channels where practicable.
